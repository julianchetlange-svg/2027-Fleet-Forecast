What McCoy's actually spends. Fuel, maintenance, depreciation, lease, registration, taxes and telematics for every asset, wherever it sits. Flat rates and mileage charges are internal and left out, so only a real change in miles, fuel or fleet size moves this number.

Moves in this scenario

Pick a new location for any asset in the table below, or terminate or relocate it out. Costs update immediately using the destination's rules. Changes are kept in this browser only; use Export to Excel to save a copy and Import scenario to load one back.

How a move works

  • The destination's rules apply. At a store an asset is charged its flat rate (and $0.425 per mile for trucks). At an actual-cost location it carries its fuel, maintenance, depreciation, lease and fees.
  • Work stays with the location. Demand is what the history shows, so a moved truck brings no new work. The miles it drove stay at its origin and pass to the trucks that remain there, at the fuel rate of the truck that left. At the destination, the location's existing miles are shared across all its trucks.
  • Forklifts carry their propane. TMFLs, trailers and other equipment have no separate fuel.
  • Terminate or relocate out. The asset's costs leave the forecast from its effective month. Terminating saves the company its maintenance, depreciation, lease and fees; relocating out moves them outside McCoy's 2027 plan. Its work stays with the remaining units at the location.
  • No store rate. Golf carts, scissor lifts, cars, van trailers and other equipment have no store flat rate. A move of one of these to a store is flagged.
  • Effective month. Every change starts in the month you pick (default January).

Method

  • Scope. Every active asset on the asset roster except 950 Rio Truss. The roster sets location and in-service date. 033 Pharr, 355CD and 355 Pharr Reload sit in Region 05; regional loaner pools (761–768) sit in their regions. 765 (Region 05) and 768 (Region 08) have no assets on the roster, so each carries one fuel-only line at the average of the other pools' fuel (2,879 gal/yr).
  • New store 122 Canyon Lake (Region 07). Opens mid-June 2027; its 9 planned assets (2 24' TA Flatbeds, 2 TMFLs, 3 6K forklifts, 1 8K forklift, 1 pickup) are charged from July. Miles, fuel and propane use the Region 07 median for each type; depreciation, maintenance and registration, taxes, Geotab and Lytx use the median of same-type units bought 2024 or later.
  • Cost rules. Stores (Regions 01–08) and regional loaner pools (761–768): fuel + flat rate, plus $0.425 per mile for trucks. Actual cost (Distribution 353/356/359/489, Millwork 450, Headquarters 730/735/900, 355CD and 355 Reload): fuel + maintenance + depreciation + lease + registration, taxes, Geotab and Lytx.
  • Truck fuel. 12 months of WEX gallons (Oct 2025–Sep 2026; diesel, gasoline and DEF combined) × the 2027 blended monthly rate. Trucks with miles but no WEX fuel use their type's median gallons per mile; trucks with no history use their type's company median miles. Distribution (353, 356, 359 incl. 489) fuel mostly from onsite tanks: the 2027 Distribution fuel forecast's diesel gallons are split across each location's trucks by miles and added to their WEX purchases, following the forecast's monthly pattern. Distribution diesel is priced at the blended rate like everyone else; its propane uses the Distribution forecast's propane prices. Calibrated to the company 2027 fuel forecast (fuel card + onsite tanks, 2,197,160 gal, $9.51M): each asset's gallons are scaled by month so the company total matches the plan's monthly gallons (factors 1.08 in Jun to 1.37 in Dec). This also covers WEX on generic cards and units no longer on the roster, about 102K gal a year.
  • Forklift fuel. AmeriGas propane gallons by location for the same months, split evenly across each location's forklifts × the propane planning price. Distribution locations use the propane gallons in the 2027 Distribution fuel forecast. Shared accounts: 033 covers 033, 355 and 355CD; 359 covers 359 and 489 EWP. The 900 airport tractor runs on Jet A, which is not tracked; it is very low use and carries no fuel. TMFLs, trailers and other equipment have no separate fuel.
  • Miles. Geotab miles Oct 2025–Sep 2026; blank months are treated as missing, and the reported-month average × 12 is used. Loaner IDs are matched on the asset number.
  • Maintenance. Vendor invoices Oct 2025–Sep 2026 (all invoice statuses, credits netted), spread across 2027 by the company-wide monthly pattern. Registration work orders are excluded where the fee file supplies registration.
  • Depreciation. Monthly amount from the March 2026 file (components summed by asset); provided costs ÷ term for new units. The in-service month is month 1; trucks and trailers stop after 60 months; forklifts, TMFLs, golf carts, scissor lifts and other equipment after 84. Leased assets get no depreciation.
  • Leases. Fixed monthly rate from the lease file; 747104 ($250) and 747106 ($150) are on holding rentals; rates not yet supplied use the 2020 Toyota batch rate of $447.11. All leases run through Dec 2027.
  • Registration, taxes, Geotab and Lytx. Monthly per asset from the company-wide fee file (all 1,441 assets). 15 assets with no original value in that file are estimated with its own formulas (taxes 0.0667% and vehicle/trailer registration 0.05% of cost per month), using the cost of the same type at the closest purchase age (same year when available); they are listed below. Charged at actual-cost locations; at stores they are built into the flat rate. The actual-cost view carries them wherever the asset sits.
